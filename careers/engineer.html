🔧 Engineer

Build amazing things with science and math!

What Does an Engineer Do?

Engineers use science and math to solve problems and build things! There are many types - some build rockets, some design video games, and some create new medicines.

Think of engineers as super problem-solvers who make the world better!

💡 Types of Engineers

Mechanical: Builds machines and moving things

Electrical: Works with electricity and electronics

Chemical: Mixes chemicals to create new products

Aerospace: Designs airplanes and spacecraft

Computer: Creates software and computer systems

🌟 Why It's a Great Job

Make a difference: Engineers solve problems that help millions of people!

High demand: Companies are always looking for engineers

Good pay: Engineers typically earn a very good salary

Creative: You get to design and create new things!

📚 How to Become an Engineer

1. Love STEM - Science, Technology, Engineering, Math!

2. Study hard in math and science classes

3. Go to college - 4 years for an engineering degree

4. Get a job - start working and learning on the job

🤔 But wait...

Engineering can be challenging - lots of math and technical classes! But if you like solving problems and building things, it's an awesome career!

💭 Remember: This is just information for kids to learn about different jobs. Talk to a parent or school counselor to learn more about any career you're interested in!
