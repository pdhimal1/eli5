🔬 Data Scientist

Use AI and math to predict the future!

What Does a Data Scientist Do?

Data scientists are like fortune tellers - but they use math and computers instead of crystal balls!

They look at past data to predict what might happen in the future. Like predicting what movie you might want to watch next on Netflix!

💡 Cool Facts!

• Data science is called "the sexiest job of the 21st century"!

• They use machine learning (a type of AI) to make predictions

• Netflix uses data science to recommend shows you'll like

🌟 Why It's a Great Job

Very high pay: One of the highest-paying tech jobs!

Super in demand: Companies desperately need data scientists!

Work on cool problems: Help doctors find diseases, help scientists discover new medicines!

Creative: You get to invent new ways to solve problems!

📚 How to Become a Data Scientist

1. Be good at math: Especially statistics and calculus

2. Learn to code: Python is the most popular language

3. Learn machine learning: How computers learn from data

4. Learn SQL: To work with databases

5. Build projects: Create your own AI models!

🤖 What can data science do?

• Recommend movies and products

• Predict stock prices

• Help doctors detect diseases early

• Self-driving cars use it to "see" the road
