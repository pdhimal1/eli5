🏠 Civil Engineer

Build houses, roads, and bridges!

What Does a Civil Engineer Do?

Civil engineers are the people who design and build everything around you - your school, the roads you drive on, bridges, dams, and even the water system!

They make sure buildings don't fall down and roads are safe to drive on.

💡 Cool Facts!

• The pyramids in Egypt were built by civil engineers!

• Civil engineering is one of the OLDEST types of engineering

• Some civil engineers helped build the Eiffel Tower!

🌟 Why It's a Great Job

Build real things: You can point to something and say "I helped build that!"

Help everyone: Everyone uses roads, bridges, and buildings every day

Good salary: Civil engineers earn a comfortable living

Always needed: Cities always need new buildings and repairs

📚 How to Become a Civil Engineer

1. Love math and science - especially geometry and physics!

2. Go to college - 4 years for a civil engineering degree

3. Get licensed - pass a test to be a Professional Engineer (PE)

4. Work on projects - start as a junior engineer and learn from senior engineers

🏗️ What do they build?

• Houses and apartments

• Roads and highways

• Bridges over rivers

• Dams to hold water

• Airports and train stations
