🎨 Graphic Designer

Create logos, posters, and beautiful art!

What Does a Graphic Designer Do?

Graphic designers create visual content! They design logos, posters, book covers, websites, ads, and anything that needs to look good.

They use computers and special software to make pictures that communicate ideas.

💡 Cool Facts!

• Graphic design is everywhere - on cereal boxes, billboards, apps!

• Popular design programs include Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva

• Some graphic designers become famous (like the person who designed the Nike swoosh!)

🌟 Why It's a Great Job

Creative: Express yourself through art every day!

In demand: Every company needs graphic designers

Work from anywhere: Many designers work remotely

Freelance: Can work for yourself and pick your own clients

📚 How to Become a Graphic Designer

1. Love art and design: Start drawing and making things!

2. Learn software: Learn programs like Photoshop and Illustrator

3. Build a portfolio: Create examples of your work to show clients

4. Get a degree (optional): Many designers go to art school

✏️ What do they design?

• Logos for companies

• Posters for movies and events

• Book and album covers

• Website layouts

• Product packaging
