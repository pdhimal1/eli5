🔥 Chatpate! 🔥

The spicy, crunchy street snack!

🌶️ 😋 🔥

What is Chatpate?

Chatpate is a SUPER popular Nepali snack!

It's a mix of crunchy bits, peanuts, and SPICY flavors!

You can buy it from street vendors everywhere!

The name means "spicy" - and they weren't kidding!

🌶️ WARNING: This snack is VERY spicy! 🌶️

What's in It?

🥜 Peanuts - roasted and crunchy!

🫘 Lentil crisps - thin and crispy!

🌶️ Chatpate masala - the spicy mix!

🧂 Salt and lime - for extra flavor!

🌿 Cilantro - fresh and herby!

🧅 Onions - chopped for crunch!

🍽️ How Do You Eat It?

You can eat chatpate:

• By itself as a snack!

• On top of chaat (fruit salad)!

• With samosas!

• Just grab a handful whenever!

Where to Get It?

Street vendors sell it everywhere!

You can buy bags at the store!

People make it at home too!

Perfect for parties and gatherings!

📝 Fun Facts!

• "Chatpate" means "spicy" in Nepali!

• It's the go-to snack while walking!

• Every vendor has their own recipe!

• Kids and adults both love it!

• You WILL need water nearby! 💧

💭 Remember: Chatpate is VERY spicy! Kids should ask a parent before trying it!
