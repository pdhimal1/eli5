🏔️ Nepali Architecture! 🏔️

Temples, stupas, and beautiful buildings!

Temples - Houses for Gods!

In Nepal, temples are special buildings where people go to pray to their gods!

They're built in many styles - some have curved roofs that look like steps!

The most famous temples are in Kathmandu (Pashupatinath, Swayambhunath, Boudhanath)!

🛕 Temple Styles

• Pagoda style: Multi-tiered roofs stacked one above another

• Shikhara style: Tower-like with a tall spire

• Stupa: Dome-shaped Buddhist monuments

• Many temples have the "Eyes of Buddha" painted on them!

🏠 Traditional Houses

In villages, houses are built from mud, brick, and wood!

The roof is slanted so rain slides off!

Some houses have multiple floors - animals on bottom, people on top!

Many have beautiful carved wooden windows!

Boudhanath - The Big Stupa!

Boudhanath is one of the largest Buddhist stupas in the world!

It has huge Buddha eyes painted on it that seem to watch over everyone!

People walk around it (called "kora") while spinning prayer wheels!

🪡 Prayer Wheels

Many Buddhist monuments have prayer wheels with mantras (holy words) written on them!

When you spin them, it's like saying the prayers!

People believe spinning them brings good luck!

💭 Remember: Many temples and stupas are sacred religious sites. Visitors should dress modestly and behave respectfully!
