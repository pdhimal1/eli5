🎊 Dashain! 🎊

The BIGGEST festival in Nepal!

What is Dashain?

Dashain is the most important festival in Nepal - a time for family, food, and celebration!

It celebrates the victory of good over evil!

The whole country shuts down for about 2 weeks so everyone can celebrate!

When is Dashain?

Dashain usually falls in September or October!

It lasts for 15 days, but the most important days are the first and last!

Families start traveling to their hometowns weeks before!

🎉 What Do People Do?

• Fly kites: The sky fills with colorful kites!

• Play cards: It's a tradition to play " Teen Paat " card game!

• Eat special food: Goat curry, rice pudding, and lots of sweets!

• Get together: Families from all over come home!

• Receive Tika: Elders put sacred rice on your forehead!

The Sacred Tika!

During Dashain, elders put "Tika" on younger family members!

Tika is a mix of rice, yogurt, and a red powder called "sindoor"!

It represents blessings for good luck and long life!

Kids also get "Eidi" - money gifts from elders!

🎁 What's Eidi?

Eidi is money that elders give to kids during Dashain!

It's like a gift and a blessing combined!

Kids look forward to collecting Eidi from all their relatives!

💭 Remember: Dashain is a cultural celebration. Different families and communities may have their own special traditions. It's a time of joy and family!
