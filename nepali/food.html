🍛 Traditional Nepali Food! 🍛

Delicious dishes from the mountains!

The National Dish - Daal Bhat!

Daal Bhat is the everyday food of Nepal - like rice and beans in other countries!

"Daal" means lentils (soup made from dried lentils), and "Bhat" means rice!

Nepalis eat Daal Bhat almost every day - for breakfast, lunch, and dinner!

🍚 What's in a Typical Daal Bhat?

• Bhat - Steamed white rice

• Daal - Lentil soup (usually with some vegetables)

• Tarkari - Vegetable curry

• Achar - Pickle/chutney (spicy!)

• Meat (optional) - Chicken, goat, or buffalo

🥟 Momos - Everyone's Favorite!

Momos are dumplings filled with meat or vegetables - likeumplings or potstickers!

They're super popular in Nepal, especially in Kathmandu!

People eat them as a snack or even for dinner!

You can get them steamed or fried!

🍜 Other Yummy Foods!

Sekuwa: Grilled meat on skewers - like BBQ!

Thukpa: Hot noodle soup, perfect for cold mountain days

Chatamari: Rice flour crepe with toppings - like a Nepali pizza!

Yomari: Sweet dumplings filled with molasses - a Newar treat!

Sel Roti: Sweet rice donut - a festival favorite!

🍵 Drinks!

• Chiya (Tea): Milk tea with spices - Nepal's national drink!

• Tongba: Warm millet drink - popular in the hills!

• Aila: Traditional rice wine - for celebrations!

💭 Remember: Food varies by region in Nepal! Terai has different food than the mountains. Also, please verify ingredients and dietary restrictions!
