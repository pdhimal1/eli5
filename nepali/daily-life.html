🙏 Daily Life in Nepal! 🙏

Customs, greetings, and everyday life!

👋 How Do People Greet Each Other?

In Nepal, people greet each other with "Namaste"!

People put their palms together like praying and say "Namaste" - it means "I bow to you"!

It's a sign of respect!

👋 Ways to Greet

• Namaste: The main greeting (palms together)

• Namaskar: Very respectful, often for elders

• Tashi Delek: Used in mountain areas (means good luck!)

• Haat: Casual greeting among friends!

🏫 School Life

Kids in Nepal go to school 5-6 days a week!

Many wear uniforms - some have cute hats!

Schools teach in Nepali or English!

After school, kids might play cricket, fly kites, or help at home!

🎮 Fun Things Kids Do

• Play cricket (super popular!)

• Fly kites (especially during festivals)

• Play "Kabaddi" (a fun tag game!)

• Watch Bollywood or Nepali movies!

🗺️ City vs Village

In cities: Kids go to school, play video games, watch TV

In villages: Kids might help with farming, collect water, tend animals

Both have their own special ways of life!

🥘 Food Culture

Nepal has lots of different foods!

People eat with their right hand (not left!)

Daal Bhat (rice and lentils) is the main food!

💭 Remember: Daily life varies by city vs village, and by community in Nepal. This is general information about common practices!
