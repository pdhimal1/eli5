🙏 Daily Life in Nepal! 🙏

Customs, greetings, and everyday life!

👋 How Do People Greet Each Other?

In Nepal, the traditional greeting is "Namaste" (or "Namaskar")!

People put their palms together like praying and say "Namaste" - it means "I bow to you"!

It's a sign of respect!

🙏 Namaste vs Namaskar

• Namaste: For everyone (from "Namah" + "te" = bow to you)

• Namaskar: Very respectful, often for elders

• Tashi Delek: Used in Buddhist areas (means good luck!)

• J时间: Very casual greeting among friends!

🏫 School Life

Kids in Nepal go to school 5-6 days a week!

Many wear uniforms - some have cute hats!

Schools teach in Nepali (or English in private schools)

After school, kids might go to temple, play cricket, or help at home!

🙏 Religion in Daily Life

Most Nepalis are Hindu or Buddhist!

Many families have a small temple (Ghar Jagaran) at home!

People ring bells at temples, light incense, and offer flowers!

Friday or Saturday is often a rest day (depends on the region)!

🌸 Special Customs

• Tika: Putting colored rice on someone's forehead for blessings

• Prasad: Holy food that's been blessed

• Parikrama: Walking around a temple or holy site

• Puja: Prayer ceremony at home or temple

🎮 Fun Things Kids Do

• Play cricket (super popular!)

• Fly kites (especially during festivals)

• Play "Kabaddi" (a fun tag game!)

• Watch Bollywood movies!

💭 Remember: Daily life varies by city vs village, and by ethnic group in Nepal. This is general information about common practices!
