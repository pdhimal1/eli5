🎊 The Big Day! 🎊

Celebrating when boys become young men!

🎓 🎉 🎈 🎁 🥳

It's Party Time!

Imagine turning 12 and having the BIGGEST birthday party ever!

In Nepali Brahmin and Chhetri families, there's a special celebration called Bartaman (or Bratabandha)!

It's like a Jewish Bar Mitzvah or Catholic First Communion - a big coming-of-age moment!

🎪 What Happens at the Party?

Family comes from everywhere! Everyone brings gifts and blessings!

There's music, dancing, amazing food, and SO much celebration!

The Ceremony Part

First, there's a small ceremony where the boy:

🛁 Takes a ritual bath - getting clean and fresh!

👔 Gets a new cotton thread draped across his chest (called Janai)!

📿 Learns some special words from the priest!

Then... PARTY MODE ACTIVATED! 🎉

🎁 What Gifts Do You Get?

Relatives give money, clothes, watches, and other cool stuff!

It's like Christmas + Birthday + your wildest dreams!

The more relatives you have, the more gifts you get!

🍽️ The Feast!

No party is complete without amazing food!

Think massive amounts of rice, curry, sweets, and treats!

Everyone eats, laughs, and has a great time!

📝 Fun Facts!

• Only boys from Brahmin and Chhetri families do this

• The ceremony is usually around ages 7-12

• It happens during a special time called "Janai Purnima" (full moon day)

• After this, the boy is considered more grown-up!

• It's a HUGE deal in the family - super proud moment!

💭 Remember: This is a celebration from specific communities in Nepal. Not all Nepali families follow this tradition. It's a cultural coming-of-age celebration - please verify with family traditions!
