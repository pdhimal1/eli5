💒 Nepali Weddings! 💒

How marriages work in Nepal!

Traditional vs Modern

In the old days, many Nepali marriages were arranged by families!

Today, many couples choose their own partners, but families still play a big role!

🎊 Before the Wedding

• Janti: The groom's family visits the bride's home with gifts

• Mehndi: Henna ceremony for the bride - beautiful patterns on hands!

• Sagai: Engagement ceremony - ring exchange!

• Decorations: The venue gets decorated with flowers and lights!

The Wedding Day!

Mandap: A special altar is set up with flowers

Pheras: The bride and groom walk around the mandap together

Sindoor: The groom puts red powder on the bride's hair part

Mangal Sutra: The groom gives the bride a sacred necklace

🎁 Fun Wedding Facts!

• The bride wears a red or traditional wedding dress with lots of jewelry!

• The groom often wears a dark suit or traditional clothes!

• Weddings can last 3-5 days in some communities!

• There's lots of singing, dancing, and eating!

Different Communities

Different ethnic groups in Nepal have their own unique wedding customs!

Newar, Gurung, Rai, and many other communities all have special traditions!

💭 Remember: Marriage traditions vary greatly by ethnicity and region in Nepal. This is general information about common practices!
