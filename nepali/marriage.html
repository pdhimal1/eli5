💒 Nepali Marriages! 💒

How weddings work in Nepal!

Traditional vs Modern

In the old days, many Nepali marriages were arranged by families - the bride and groom might not even meet before the wedding!

Today, many couples choose their own partners, but families still play a big role!

Both Hindu and Buddhist traditions are followed, depending on the family's religion.

🎊 Before the Wedding

• Janti: The groom's family visits the bride's home with gifts

• Mahindi: Henna/ Mehndi ceremony for the bride

• Sagai: Engagement ceremony - ring exchange!

• Door decorations: The bride's house gets decorated with flowers!

The Wedding Day!

Mandap: A special altar is set up with a fire burning

Gatte: The bride and groom walk around the fire 7 times

Sindoor: The groom puts red powder on the bride's hair part

Mang: The bride puts a necklace (mala) around the groom's neck

🎁 Fun Wedding Facts!

• The bride wears a red sari with lots of jewelry!

• The groom often wears a dark suit or traditional daura suruwal!

• Weddings can last 3-5 days in some communities!

• There's lots of singing, dancing, and eating!

• The bride's family gives a "dowry" (gifts) to the groom's family (controversial!)

Different Communities

Newar: The bride and groom drink from the same cup during the ceremony!

Gurung/Rai: Have unique dance performances during weddings!

Buddhist: May have Buddhist monks performing ceremonies!

💭 Remember: Marriage traditions vary greatly by ethnicity, religion, and region in Nepal. This is general information about common practices!
