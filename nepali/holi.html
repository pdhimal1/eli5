🎨 Holi! 🎨

The Festival of Colors - the most colorful day of the year!

What is Holi?

Holi is the festival of colors! It's when everyone throws colored powder and water at each other!

It's a celebration of spring, friendship, and having fun!

Everyone looks like a rainbow by the end of the day!

When is Holi?

Holi usually falls in March - it's a spring festival!

The main color-throwing day is called "Phagu" or "Holi" day!

The day before is called "Chhath" or "Holika Dahan" - when people light bonfires!

🎨 How Do People Celebrate?

• Throw colors: People use "abeer" (colorful powder) and water balloons!

• Play music: There are drums and dancing in the streets!

• Visit friends: People go to each other's houses to celebrate!

• Eat special food: Gujiya (sweet dumplings) and drinks!

Fun in Nepal!

In Nepal, Holi is especially fun in Thamel (the tourist area of Kathmandu)!

Everyone - locals and tourists - gets covered in colors!

It's one of the most joyful days of the year!

💦 The Colors!

People use:

• Dry colored powder (gulal)

• Colored water

• Water balloons

• Water guns!

The colors represent the arrival of spring!

💭 Remember: Holi is celebrated by many communities in Nepal, but not everyone participates. It's a fun, joyful celebration of spring and friendship!
