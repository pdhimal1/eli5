🪔 Tihar! 🪔

The Festival of Lights - celebrating right after Dashain!

What is Tihar?

Tihar is a beautiful festival of lights that comes right after Dashain!

It's a 5-day festival where people light candles and oil lamps everywhere!

The whole country glows at night - it's magical!

When is Tihar?

Tihar comes about 2 weeks after Dashain, usually in October or November!

It lasts for 5 days, and each day honors something different!

🐦 Day 1 - Kag Tihar (Crow)

People honor crows - they're seen as messengers. Families put out food for crows in the morning!

🐕 Day 2 - Kukur Tihar (Dogs)

Dogs get special treats and garlands! Dogs are considered helpers of humans and are honored!

🐄 Day 3 - Gai Tihar (Cows)

Cows are decorated with flowers and fed special food. Cows are sacred in Nepal!

🧑 Day 4 - Govardhan Puja / Laxmi Puja

This day honors cows (Govardhan) or the goddess of wealth (Laxmi). People clean their houses and light lots of lamps!

👨 Day 5 - Bhai Tika (Brother's Day)

Sisters put Tika on their brothers' foreheads and give them gifts. Brothers give gifts back to sisters!

🪔 The Lights!

People decorate with:

• Oil lamps (Diyo)

• Candles

• Flower garlands

• Colored powder ( Rangoli ) on the ground!

💭 Remember: Tihar is a cultural celebration. Different communities may celebrate different days or in different ways!
