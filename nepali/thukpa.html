🍜 Thukpa! 🍜

Warm noodle soup from the mountains!

🍜 🥣 ❤️

What is Thukpa?

Thukpa is a hot, hearty noodle soup!

It's perfect for cold mountain days!

It came from Tibet and spread through Nepal's mountain regions!

It's like a warm hug in a bowl!

What's in It?

🍜 Noodles - stretched or cut into strips!

🥩 Meat - usually chicken, yak, or buff!

🥬 Vegetables - spinach, cabbage, carrots!

🍳 Eggs - sometimes added!

🌶️ Spices - to make it warm and tasty!

💪 Perfect for Cold Days!

If you live in the mountains of Nepal, thukpa is the best!

When it's snowing outside, nothing beats a warm bowl!

Sherpas and mountain people eat it a lot!

It's comfort food at its finest!

Types of Thukpa!

Thukpa: Noodle soup with meat and veggies!

Thenthuk: Hand-pulled noodle soup!

Gyathuk: Similar but with different noodles!

Each region has its own special version!

📝 Fun Facts!

• "Thuk" means "noodle" in Tibetan!

• Sherpas brought it to Nepal!

• It's especially popular in winter!

• Each family has their own secret recipe!

• Some versions are super spicy!

💭 Remember: Thukpa is from the mountain regions of Nepal. Recipes vary by family and region!
