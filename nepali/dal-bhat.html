What's Daal Bhat?

Daal Bhat is the national dish of Nepal!

"Daal" = lentil soup (made from dried lentils)

"Bhat" = steamed rice

Together = the most important meal in Nepal!

The Full Plate!

A proper Daal Bhat meal comes with:

🍚 Bhat - fluffy white rice

🥣 Daal - warm lentil soup

🥘 Tarkari - vegetable curry

🌶️ Achar - spicy pickle/chutney

🍗 (Optional) Meat - chicken, goat, or buffalo

💪 Why is it SO Popular?

• It's super healthy! Lentils have protein, rice gives energy!

• Nepalis eat it TWICE a day - lunch AND dinner!

• It's cheap and filling!

• There's a saying: "Daal Bhat power, 24 hour!"

• Once you start eating Daal Bhat, you can't stop!

How to Eat Like a Nepali!

Mix the daal with your rice!

Add some tarkari on the side!

Don't forget the achar for some kick!

Eat with your right hand (traditionally)!

And ALWAYS finish everything on your plate!

💭 Remember: Food varies by region! Mountain areas have different food than the Terai (plains). This is general Nepali food info!
