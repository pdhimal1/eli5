🎉 Festivals of Nepal! 🎉

The land of festivals - 50+ celebrations a year!

Why Nepal Has So Many Festivals!

Nepal is called the "land of festivals" because there are over 50 festivals celebrated every year!

Different communities and ethnic groups all have their own special celebrations!

Many festivals celebrate nature, family, and the changing seasons!

🎊

Dashain

The biggest festival of all!
🪔

Tihar

Festival of lights and honoring animals!
🎨

Holi

The festival of colors!

💭 Remember: Nepal has many different communities. Different families and ethnic groups may celebrate festivals in their own special ways!
