🎉 Festivals of Nepal! 🎉

The land of festivals - 50+ celebrations a year!

Why Nepal Has So Many Festivals!

Nepal is called the "land of festivals" because there are over 50 festivals celebrated every year!

Different ethnic groups (like Newar, Brahmin, Chhetri, Tamang, and others) all have their own special celebrations.

Many festivals are based on the Hindu and Buddhist calendars, which follow the moon and stars!

🎊 Dashain - The Biggest Festival!

Dashain is the most important festival in Nepal, celebrating the victory of good over evil!

It remembers when goddess Durga defeated a buffalo demon named Mahishasura.

Families get together, eat special food, play cards, and kids get "Eidi" (money gifts) from elders!

When: Usually September/October, lasts 15 days

Dashain Activities

• Receiving "Tika" - a mix of rice, yogurt, and vermillion on the forehead

• Getting "Eidi" - money gifts from elders

• Flying kites

• Playing cards (a tradition during Dashain!)

• Eating special foods like goat curry and rice pudding

🪔 Tihar - Festival of Lights!

Tihar comes right after Dashain and is all about honoring animals and the goddess of wealth!

It's like a Thanksgiving and Diwali combined!

When: Usually October/November, lasts 5 days

Tihar - Day by Day

• Day 1: Crows - feed them (they're messengers of death)

• Day 2: Dogs - honor them (they guard the gates of heaven)

• Day 3: Cows - decorate them with flowers and garlands

• Day 4: Oxen or tonight is Laxmi Puja - the goddess of wealth!

• Day 5: Brothers get gifts from sisters (Bhai Tika!)

🎨 Other Fun Festivals!

Holi: The festival of colors! People throw colored powder and water at each other!

Indra Jatra: A Newar festival with masked dances and the living goddess!

Buddha Jayanti: Celebration of Buddha's birth (in Lumbini!)

Shivaratri: Night of Lord Shiva - people stay awake all night!

💭 Remember: This is general information about Nepali festivals. Different families and ethnic groups may have their own unique traditions and ways of celebrating!
