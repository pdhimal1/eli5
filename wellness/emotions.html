😊 Understanding Emotions! 😊

All feelings are valid!

🌈 Feelings Are Like Weather!

Emotions are like weather in your heart - sometimes sunny, sometimes rainy!

All emotions are okay to feel! There's no "bad" emotion - they all tell us something important!

Happy tells us we enjoy something. Sad tells us we miss something. Angry tells us something isn't fair!

🎮 Emotion Explorer!

🎭 Basic Emotions!

😊 Happy: You feel good and content!

😢 Sad: You feel down or miss someone/something!

😠 Angry: Something feels unfair or frustrating!

😨 Scared: You feel danger or unsafe!

😲 Surprised: Something unexpected happened!

💡 Why We Have Emotions!

Emotions are like superpowers that help us:

🛡️ Protect ourselves: Fear keeps us safe!

🤝 Connect with others: Sharing feelings builds friendships!

🎯 Make decisions: Our gut feeling helps us choose!

💜 It's okay to feel all your emotions! Talking about feelings helps them feel smaller.
