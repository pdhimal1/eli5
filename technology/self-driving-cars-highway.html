🛣️ Highway Driving Assistant 🛣️

How cars help you drive on highways!

What is Highway Driving Assistant?

Highway driving assistant is a feature in modern cars that helps you drive safely on highways! It can steer, speed up, and slow down for you!

It's like having a co-pilot that never gets tired!

🚗 What Can It Do?

Lane Keeping: Keeps your car in the lane

Adaptive Cruise Control: Automatically slows down if the car ahead slows

Automatic Braking: Stops if it sees something in front

Blind Spot Warning: Tells you if someone is in your blind spot

👀 How Does It See?

Most self-driving cars use a MIX of different sensors to "see" the road:

📷 Cameras: Like human eyes, they see colors and shapes

🔴 Radar: Uses radio waves to detect objects, works in rain and fog

💡 Lidar: Uses lasers to create a 3D map of the world

By combining all three, the car gets the best of all worlds!

⚠️ Important - Still Needs a Driver!

Even though these features are amazing, you still need to pay attention! The car might not see everything.

Always keep your hands on the wheel and be ready to take over at any time!
