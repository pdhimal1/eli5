🏢 Data Centers 🏢

The buildings that power the internet!

What is a Data Center?

A data center is a special building filled with thousands of computers that store data and run the internet!

When you watch a YouTube video, check Instagram, or play a video game, the data comes from a data center!

💡 Cool Facts!

• There are millions of servers in big data centers!

• Data centers use as much electricity as some small countries!

• They're kept very cold to prevent computers from overheating

• Most data centers have backup generators in case the power goes out

🔌 What Keeps Them Running?

Servers: These are the special computers that store data

Cooling: Huge AC systems keep everything cold

Power: Multiple power sources and backup generators

Security: Guards, cameras, and locked doors protect the data

🌍 Where Are They?

Data centers are all over the world! Companies like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft have hundreds of them.

Some are in cold places (to save on cooling costs), some underground (for security), and some in remote areas.
