🥞 Perfect Pancakes! 🥞

Fluffy, yummy breakfast treat!

⚠️ Safety: Ask adult to help with hot stove!

🥣 The Easy Way - Use a Mix!

Making pancakes from scratch is cool, but for kids, pancake mix is way easier! No measuring lots of ingredients - just add water and cook!

We love Birch Bender's Complete Pancake & Waffle Mix, organic buttermilk - just add water and that's it!

🛒 What You Need

  • 1 cup Birch Bender's Complete Pancake & Waffle Mix
  • ¾ cup water
  • Butter for the pan (makes it super tasty!)

👨‍🍳 How to Make Pancakes (Super Easy!)

Step 1: Put some butter in the pan and let it melt

Step 2: Pour the mix into a bowl

Step 3: Add water (check the box for how much!)

Step 4: Stir until just mixed - lumps are okay!

Step 5: Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake

Step 6: Wait for bubbles to form, then flip!

Step 7: Cook 1-2 more minutes until golden

Step 8: Add more butter on top and eat!

💡 Pro Tips:
• Don't overmix! Lumps make fluffy pancakes!
• Medium heat is key - too hot = burnt outside, raw inside
• Bubbles that pop and stay open = ready to flip!
• Butter on the pan AND butter on top = extra yummy!

🍯 Fun Toppings

Classic: Maple syrup and butter

Fruity: Fresh berries or sliced bananas

Nutty: Peanut butter or almond butter

Sweet: Whipped cream and chocolate chips

Savory: Bacon and cheese

❓ Why Mix Instead of From Scratch?

Less mess: Fewer bowls to clean!

Easier: No measuring lots of different ingredients

Perfect every time: The experts already figured out the recipe!

Great for kids: You can do it mostly by yourself!
